# Agnify

**AI video analytics on the cameras you already have.**

[Agnify](https://agnify.ai) connects to existing factory and warehouse cameras and runs computer vision and vision-language models on the feeds. Plants get machine downtime with the clip, cycle time by station, quality checks and safety alerts, without new sensors.

- 🏭 [Manufacturing operations](https://agnify.ai/operations/): downtime, station utilization, step checks
- ⏱️ [Machine downtime tracking](https://agnify.ai/equipment-monitoring/): a reason and a clip for every stop
- 🔩 [Metal fabrication](https://agnify.ai/metal-fabrication/): welding cell and fab shop utilization
- 🚚 [Logistics](https://agnify.ai/logistics/): dock doors, trailer dwell, forklift safety
- 🎬 [Examples](https://agnify.ai/examples/) and [video analysis](https://agnify.ai/video/)
